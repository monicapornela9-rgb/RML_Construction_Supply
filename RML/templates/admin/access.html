Admin System Governance
Administrator

Full enterprise control. Can assign roles, manage accounts, authorize dispatch deliveries, replenish inventory, verify payments, and export reports.

Key Capabilities:
  • • User management & role assignment
  • • Order scheduling & status approvals
  • • Truck dispatch & driver assignment
  • • Catalog pricing & inventory control
  • • Payment verification & business reports
Driver Field Operations
Delivery Driver

Restricted field interface. Can view and update assigned dispatches, update live GPS, record recipient acknowledgement, and upload delivery proof.

Boundary Protections:
  • • View assigned delivery stops only
  • • GPS tracking & status updates
  • • Collect digital proof & recipient signature
  • • No access to customer database
  • • No access to inventory or financial reports
Customer Self-Service Storefront
Customer

Public purchasing portal. Can browse catalog, place orders, choose preferred delivery time slots, upload payment proof, and track real-time delivery dispatches.

Customer Workspaces:
  • • Product discovery & shopping cart
  • • Order placement & slot reservation
  • • Proof of payment upload (QR/Bank)
  • • Order status & dispatch tracking
  • • Personal profile & security management
