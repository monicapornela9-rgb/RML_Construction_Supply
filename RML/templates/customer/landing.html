Privacy Policy · Terms and Conditions
Privacy Policy

RML Construction Supply values customer privacy and complies with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Personal details provided during customer registration, such as contact numbers, email addresses, and delivery addresses, are securely processed solely for order fulfillment, payment verification, and fleet dispatch delivery services.

Delivery vehicle GPS telemetry is visible only to authorized accounts and is not shared with third parties.

Terms and Conditions

1. Ordering & Delivery Scheduling: All orders are subject to inventory availability and admin dispatch verification. Proposed delivery time slots (Morning or Afternoon) must be accepted by the customer before order confirmation.

2. Payment Verification: For QR code or bank transfers, payment proofs must be verified by depot administration prior to delivery dispatch. Cash on Delivery (COD) is collected upon site arrival.

3. Unloading & Site Access: The customer is responsible for ensuring clear, accessible roadways suitable for cargo and boom trucks at the specified delivery site.
